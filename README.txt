Ancillary files for "An explicit 13-stage Runge-Kutta method of order 9 with exact algebraic coefficients"

These files let a reader recheck the theorem without trusting the software that found the method. Run every command
below from this folder (the one that holds scripts/ and results/). SHA256SUMS lists the SHA-256 digest of every file.

THE PROOF

The pure-Python check needs only a Python 3 interpreter (no packages):

  python3 scripts/purepy_exact_check.py results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json

It builds the 13-stage tableau over the field K of degree 16 from the exact data and checks all 486 order conditions
with at most 9 nodes in exact rational arithmetic. It also runs the controls (tree counts, classical RK4, a
perturbed tableau that must fail) and isolates the 8 real roots of F with the sign of the discriminant D at each. On
the author's laptop it took 67.0 s and 23 MB with CPython 3.12.7.

DATA (results/)

  sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json
      The exact data: the fixed and free coordinates, the tower form (F, P0, P1 and the pair (u, v) for every free
      coordinate) and the univariate form (f and the g_j, with x_j = g_j(theta) / f'(theta)). SHA-256
      eb10c24c6c683841f4b920dcc1b84fae4bc6fac6eaf2066c5096de471bc859e3, as printed in the paper.
  fibre_sigma_inner1_certificate.json
      Output of fibre_verify.py: f, the verification flags and the 16 isolating intervals of the real roots of f,
      each with rational endpoints, a sign change of f and Sturm count 1.
  sigma_inner1_tableaux_16_embeddings.json
      The tableau and its nodes at each of the 16 real embeddings, to 30 significant digits, from arb ball arithmetic
      at 120 digits on intervals narrowed by exact bisection. Every ball has relative radius below 1e-35, so all 30
      printed digits are certified. Embedding index 10 is the one printed in the paper's table. The order-condition
      residuals recorded there are a numerical check, not part of the proof.
  node_degrees_exact.json
      Exact minimal polynomials over Q of the 13 nodes, written by node_degrees_exact.py; input of subfield_test.py.
  refined_S1.txt
      Khashin's 2009 tableau after Newton refinement at 250 digits (start S1 in the paper). Numerical; not part of the
      proof.

proofs/fibre_sigma_inner1_certificate.md is a human-readable form of the certificate file.

THE OTHER CHECKS (scripts/)

These need python-flint (the author used python-flint 0.9.0, with FLINT 3.6.0), and some need mpmath (1.3.0) or
numpy. The indep_exact_* programs were written from Butcher's definitions by an agent that did not read the tree,
order-condition, tableau or verification code of the other programs and saw only the layout of the data file.

  python3 scripts/indep_exact_controls.py
      Tree counts against OEIS A000081, classical RK4, self-test of the field arithmetic.
  python3 scripts/indep_exact_tower.py results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json
      The 486 conditions in the tower form, irreducibility, a perturbation control and one real embedding
      (145.7 s on the author's laptop).
  python3 scripts/indep_exact_univariate.py results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json
      The 486 conditions in the univariate form, with f' inverted by the extended Euclidean algorithm, and an exact
      sweep over the 16 real roots (2197.8 s, almost all of it the sweep).
  python3 scripts/indep_exact_field.py results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json
      K is a field of degree 16 over Q.
  python3 scripts/fibre_verify.py --rur results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json --tag rerun
      The lane's verifier of the univariate form; writes results/fibre_rerun_certificate.json and
      proofs/fibre_rerun_certificate.md, which should agree with the deposited certificate.
  python3 scripts/structure_exact_check.py results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json
      The structural properties of the paper (stage orders, D(zeta), coincidences of nodes) hold exactly in K.
  python3 scripts/node_degrees_exact.py results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json
      Degrees of the nodes over Q; rewrites results/node_degrees_exact.json.
  python3 scripts/subfield_test.py results/node_degrees_exact.json c3 c12
      Whether one node lies in the field generated by another (Trager's norm), for the pairs discussed in the paper.
  python3 scripts/paper_tableaux16.py results/sigma_rur_inner1_tower.json results/fibre_sigma_inner1_certificate.json
      Rewrites results/sigma_inner1_tableaux_16_embeddings.json.

order_conditions.py, fibre_common.py and indep_exact_core.py are modules used by the scripts above.
